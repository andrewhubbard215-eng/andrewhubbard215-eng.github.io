This is the campus product. Lincoln marks, HCR codes, Clock In for a class. Separate from the Google Play listing, which is HVAC Allstars with no school name.

Two products

  • Campus — Lincoln Tech edition. Instructor desk + Clock In with school marks. Can be leased by the seat if a campus wants it.
  • Store — HVAC Allstars on Google Play. Unbranded. No Lincoln Tech in the title, icon, or screenshots.

What a seat is

One student on the roster for a term. They get Clock In, films, 608 drills, electrical, sandbox. The instructor keeps roll, sheets, labs, print packets.

What it is not

  • Not official Lincoln Technical Institute curriculum or copyrighted courseware.
  • Not the live EPA / ESCO exam bank.
  • Not a signed contract. This page is the product split. Terms go on paper when you lease.
