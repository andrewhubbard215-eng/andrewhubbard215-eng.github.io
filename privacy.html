Privacy policy

Last updated September 11, 2026. This is a vocational training app, not a data vacuum.

What stays on your device

Callsign, locker photo, password hash, XP, campus, exam scores, and lab progress live in this browser’s storage (localStorage). We do not upload your selfie or password to a server we control. If you clear site data, the locker is gone.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising ID, no cross-app tracking, no sale of student data.
  • No location beyond what you type as campus.
  • No real explosive-device content. “Callback bomb” means a Saturday HVAC no-cool.

Classroom PIN / online arena

If you join a class PIN, your callsign and scores may be shown to that class on the same device network or the classroom host. Don’t put a legal last name or student ID in the callsign field.

Photos

Label-scan and unit photos you feed Professor HUB are processed on-device when possible. Do not photograph customers, kids, or nameplates you don’t have permission to use.

Kids

Built for trade students and working techs. Not directed at children under 13. Google Play content rating: Teen (mild language in optional HUB roast).

Contact

Support: support page. Campus edition questions: your instructor. Store edition: the developer listing on Google Play.
