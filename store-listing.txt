Full description
HVAC Allstars is a vocational shop trainer for HVAC students and working techs.

Clock In on the shop floor. Run the refrigeration sandbox. Land lugs on a real electrical box. Sit EPA 608 drills (Core / Type I / II / III) without dumping the live federal bank. Watch component film — host first, then internals — oil, gas, hydronic, cycle.

Voice is Rex. Short sentences. Prove it with a meter.

This is instructor-created practice, not official school curriculum and not ESCO or EPA exam questions.
